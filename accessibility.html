Our Commitment

KUKPO Holdings is committed to ensuring that kukpoholdings.com is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. "Systems for people" is our founding philosophy, and that includes making our own website usable by all. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

Accessibility Features on This Site

  • A "Skip to content" link for keyboard and screen-reader users
  • Full keyboard operability with visible focus indicators
  • Semantic HTML landmarks (header, navigation, main, footer) and a logical heading structure
  • ARIA labels on interactive controls, including the AI chat assistant
  • A pause/play control for the decorative background video
  • Support for the "reduced motion" operating-system preference — animations, moving text, and background video are disabled automatically when enabled
  • Color contrast designed to meet WCAG AA ratios for text
  • Responsive layout that supports zoom up to 200% and mobile screen readers

Known Limitations

Some decorative visual effects (such as the 3D background graphics) are purely aesthetic and are hidden from assistive technologies. If you encounter any content that is difficult to access, please let us know.

Feedback

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of this Site. If you experience any barriers or have suggestions, please contact us at info@kukpoholdings.com. We try to respond within 2 business days and will work to resolve issues promptly.
